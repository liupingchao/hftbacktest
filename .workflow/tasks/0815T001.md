primary episode-merging contract：
- Version: `episode_merging_v1`.
- Every Family A candidate receives one candidate ID, primary ShockCluster,
  ContinuousFlowEpisode, 2000ms overlap-block ID and connection epoch.
- Within one segment/epoch, consecutive candidates with gap `<=100ms` join
  one ShockCluster.
- Adjacent clusters bridge only when gap `<=250ms` and no accepted recovery
  checkpoint exists.
- Recovery requires at least two Binance states spanning `>=50ms`, spread no
  wider than pre-state plus one tick, combined top-5 depth at least `80%` of
  pre-state, and no extension of the directional midpoint extreme for
  `>=50ms`.
- Missing/ambiguous recovery evidence means no bridge.
- Segment and connection-epoch boundaries terminate every cluster and block.
- Candidate outcome windows are structural intervals
  `[shock_ts_ns, min(shock_ts_ns+2000ms, segment_end)]`; overlapping windows
  belong to the same overlap block. No outcome value is opened.

  - same-side refractory `100ms`, `250ms`, `500ms`;

effective-support contract：
- Publish raw candidate count, confirmed count, ShockCluster count,
  ContinuousFlowEpisode count, 2000ms overlap-block count and 60-second
  segment-contained time-block count separately.
- `overlap_block_count_2000ms` is the structural non-overlap unit required by
  the plan; it is never replaced by row count.
- `time_block_count_60s` uses bins anchored at each segment start and never
  crosses segment/epoch boundaries.
- Publish Bartlett ESS for 1-second candidate-count and confirmed-count
  series, calculated independently per segment and summed by session:

\[
\tau
=
1+2\sum_k \rho_k,
\qquad
N_{eff}=\min(N,\max(1,N/\tau))
\]

- Use lags up to `60s` and Geyer initial-positive-pair truncation: stop before
  the first pair
  `rho_(2k-1) + rho_(2k) <= 0`. Constant/too-short series fail closed to an
  explicit unavailable reason rather than inventing an ESS.
- Do not publish one universal `N_eff`; publish estimator name, population,
  segment contribution, session total, assumptions and availability.
